01
LAW 1
Value>opinions

THE LAW
Generally speaking, audiences are selfish until they trust you.
They care about what's in it for them. Why should they trade attention for
your content?
Give more to the "attention pie" than you take.

The "attention pie"
What you give: value, insight, help
What you take: attention, follows, sales

Before you post, ask: what does the reader walk away with? If the answer is "my
opinion", add the value or don't post it.


02
LAW 2
Masterthe hero's journey

THE LAW
Your reader is the hero. You're just the guide who's already
walked the path.
Stories beat lectures. Put your reader at the centre of the story and position
yourself as the guide who helps them get from where they are to where they
want to be.

THE HERO'S JOURNEY, APPLIED TO YOUR CONTENT

1. Ordinary world
Where your reader is now

2. The problem
What's holding them back

3. Meets the guide
They discover you

4. The struggle
They try, fail, push through

5. Transformation (product)
They get the result

6. New normal
And they trust you

Map your next post to the above: 
Think about their ordinary world, their problem, and the result. You show up at stage 3.



03
LAW 3
CTA everything

THE LAW
Assume nobody knows who you are. CTA everything.
Want someone to try your product? Ask for it.
Want a newsletter sign-up? Ask for it.
People need to be told what to do next.

EXAMPLE: THE CTA REPLY UNDER A VIRAL POST

When a post takes off, reply under it with one clear next step: follow, subscribe, or DM
a keyword.


04
LAW 4
Every post needs a job

THE LAW
Before you post anything, ask: what is this post supposed to
do?

Posts have three main jobs:
a. Reach: gets you in front of new people
b. Trust: proves you know what you're talking about
c. Convert: turns followers into customers

COPY DETAIL BY FUNNEL STAGE
TOF Reach : Short & punchy: Broad audience, quick value
MOF Trust: More depth: Proof, examples, stories
BOF Sale: Most detailed Specifics + clear CTA
The closer to a sale, the more detail you need.

match the level of
detail to the job.


05
LAW 5
Clarity>clever

THE LAW
Say exactly what you mean, in the simplest words possible.
Your goal isn't to sound smart. It's to get the viewer to understand and
resonate.

CLARITY > CLEVERNESS · RUN EVERY POST THROUGH THIS BEFORE YOU DRAFT
✓ One idea per post
Two ideas = zero ideas remembered.

✓ Simple words over smart ones
If a 12 year old wouldn't get it, rewrite it.

✓ Lead with the outcome
Say what they get in the first line.

✓ Specifics, not metaphors
Numbers, names, steps. Not poetry.

✓ Cut every extra word
If the line works without it, delete it.

✓ Pass the skim test
Understood in 2 seconds or it gets scrolled.


Run your next draft through the six checks above. Fix every one it fails before you publish.

LAW 12
Two routes to credibility and
trust

Route 1: Post with proof. You've done the thing. Share the results, proof, and
teach how you did it.
Route 2: Build in public. You're actively doing the thing. Share the journey,

LAW 13
Packaging is 90%

THE LAW
Spend at least half your time on the title, image, hook, and
promise. The content only matters if someone opens it.
A good idea wrapped in sh*t packaging will flop.

HOW TO SPLIT YOUR TIME ON EVERY POST

50%+ packaging
Title · image · hook · promise
